01 / About SAHARA

Good intentions.
Real-world action.

We are the Social Welfare Society of the IIT Madras BS Degree community: students finding ways to show up for people, animals, and the world we share.

✳

Care begins with
paying attention.

See compassion in action ↗
01

Our mission

Bring students closer to social welfare through awareness, thoughtful conversations, and hands-on volunteering. Make compassion something we practise together.

02

Our vision

A student community where contributing to society becomes a lasting habit. Where learning from one another helps us respond to real needs with empathy and care.

03

Our approach

Learn from social activists. Work alongside welfare organizations. Create space for students to contribute their time, ideas, and skills beyond the classroom.

What impact looks like

A conversation that opens a mind. Time spent with a rescued animal. A child encouraged to create. Small moments are where meaningful change begins.
