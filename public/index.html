About Us

A society built on
compassion.

We are the Social Welfare Society of the IIT Madras BS Degree community: students finding ways to show up for people, animals, and the world we share.

Join Us (opens in a new tab)

Our goals

Compassion with a practical purpose.

  • Make a difference in real lives. Give students meaningful ways to support people and animals through practical service.
  • Build awareness. Open conversations about social welfare and recognize the people doing the work.
  • Take part in welfare activities. Learn first-hand by volunteering with established organizations.
  • Connect willing students with real needs. Make it easier to turn an intention to help into a shared effort.

What we do

Different ways to contribute.

  1. Offline volunteering

    Work alongside NGOs, meet fellow BS students, and learn through service and shared travel.

  2. Guest conversations

    Ask social activists and welfare practitioners about their work and everyday choices.

  3. Solution-focused competitions

    Use posters, art, debates, and writing to explore social issues.

  4. Social awareness content

    Share ideas that encourage empathy, better understanding, and thoughtful action.

Awareness, beyond an event

Ideas worth sharing.

Our awareness series include Myth Buster, Health Tips, and Know Your Revolutionary (KYR), which introduces welfare organizations and their founders.

Follow the society’s official channels for its published content.

Past activity · 1–2 October 2023

Udaipur:
care for rescued animals.

Animal Aid Unlimited · Rajasthan

  • Animal Aid Unlimited
  • Boundless
  • Synapse

More than 30 students joined the society’s first offline activity. Guided by the AAU team, they learned about rescue work, spent time with animals, and practised compassionate handling.

  • Supported AAU through donations and merchandise purchases.
  • Combined volunteering with a shared exploration of Udaipur.
  • Visited Pichola Lake, Fateh Sagar Lake, Bahubali Hills, Gangaur and Ambrai Ghats, Jagdish Temple, and Karni Mata Temple.

Past activity · 21–22 November 2023

Indore:
Huge Smiles, Little Hearts.

Robin Hood Army · Madhya Pradesh

  • Robin Hood Army
  • Boundless
  • Synapse

At Kali Basti, Bhavarkua, students joined the Robin Hood Army for a painting competition and conversations with children. The published account records stationery for 30 children, funded through nearly ₹3,500 in community contributions.

  • Distributed food packets, stationery, and chocolates.
  • Talked about personal safety, menstruation, and career aspirations.
  • Explored Lotus Valley, Pitra Parvat, Indore Zoo, Central Museum, Rajwada Palace, and other city landmarks.
